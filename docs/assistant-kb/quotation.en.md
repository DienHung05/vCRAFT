# Ordering in bulk with a quote request

To order many pieces, for example 5,000 cups with a logo, sign in with a buyer account and open "Quote requests" → "New request" at /en/quotes/new. Describe the design, quantity, unit, delivery place, the date you need it, the bidding deadline and your budget if you have one. The request starts as a draft; press Submit to send it to the households.

You can invite households yourself or ask the village board to coordinate: the board invites suitable households, combines their offers into one cooperative quotation and sends it to you to compare.

Each household's offer stays private; you only see the combined quotation. Once you accept one, the households confirm their shares, the order goes into production and you follow progress on the request page.

If the bidding deadline passes with no offer, the request expires; with offers, you have 7 days to choose.
