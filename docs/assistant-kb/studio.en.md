# The household Studio

The Studio (/en/studio) is the workspace of an approved household's representative. Use /en/studio/products to publish a new product, edit or withdraw one; each product has its own page /en/studio/products/ID for details, photos and the makers credited.

The production journal lives on the product page: add entries by stage (shaping, drying, painting, glazing, firing) with the date, a note and photos. Once published, an entry is only corrected by a later correction entry, never deleted.

Quote invitations appear on /en/studio/quotes: read the request, send a contribution offer (maximum quantity, unit price, completion date) or decline. The household's share of orders is on /en/studio/orders: confirm or decline each allocation, then report progress with the completed quantity and photos.

The household profile is /en/studio/profile: name, story, workshops and their places on the map. A household that is pending or rejected only sees this application-status page; a suspended household does too.

Note: a suspended household keeps its private records while its public presence is hidden; a withdrawn product also leaves the exhibition.
