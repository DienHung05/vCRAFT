# The village board area (Admin)

The village board works in /en/admin. The dashboard /en/admin gathers the numbers: assistant usage, recent activity and village statistics.

Household approvals: /en/admin/approvals lists pending applications; open one at /en/admin/approvals/ID to read the declared details, documents and the representative's citizen ID number (visible through the dedicated reveal control), the workshop map, then approve, reject or suspend the household.

Quote coordination: /en/admin/coordination is the queue of requests the board was asked to coordinate; open /en/admin/coordination/CODE to invite households, read each household's contribution offer and assemble one cooperative quotation for the buyer. The progress board /en/admin/progress tracks every order's allocations (late, running, done) and lets the board reallocate a household's share.

Village news: /en/admin/news to publish, edit or remove items; public news shows on /en/news, household-only news reaches approved representatives only. The audit log /en/admin/audit-log records every administrative action.

The Fox only explains these pages; it never approves or edits anything for you, every action must be done by an administrator account.
