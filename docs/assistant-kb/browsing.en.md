# Buying a few pieces and finding products

All products are listed on /en/products with filters and keyword search; suggestions appear as you type. The home page also features a few products and households.

A product page /en/products/SLUG shows the price in dong (the English site adds an approximate dollar price), how many pieces are left, the material, the size, the people who made it and the household selling it. A piece marked "One of a kind" is a single object; batch products have several pieces of the same design.

Approved households are listed on /en/artisans; a household's page /en/artisans/SLUG tells its story, its workshops, visiting hours and the products on sale.

The village map /en/map pins the workshops, shows which are open now and their visiting hours, with a list mode when the map cannot load.

Village news and activity live on /en/news, including material prices and power outages. Photo sources are on /en/image-credits; the data policy is on /en/privacy-policy.
