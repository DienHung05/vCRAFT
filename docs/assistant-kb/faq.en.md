# Frequently asked questions

How do I use vCraft? See "How to use vCraft"; the Fox can summarise the steps for your role.

I just want a couple of vases. Open the product page and press "Request a quote" or contact the household from its page. For hundreds or thousands of pieces use a quote request at /en/quotes/new.

Cannot sign in? Check that you confirmed your email; a forgotten password is reset at /en/forgot-password. A locked account only sees a notice page.

Why is my household not in the Studio yet? The village board must approve it first; follow the status on /en/studio/profile. "Pending review" means the board is reading it; "Rejected" and "Suspended" both leave only the profile page visible.

What is the QR code on my piece for? Scanning it opens the trace page /en/trace/CODE: who made the piece, the stages and the journal. A code that does not exist, or whose household is suspended from the exhibition, has no available public trace record. This does not determine whether the physical object is genuine.

When can I pay online? Under ADR-0021 the feature is in a sandbox phase and not open for real orders yet.

What can the Fox do? The Fox only reads: it explains, finds products and links the right page. It never places orders, sends quote requests or sees anyone's private data.
