# Accounts on vCraft

Create an account on the Register page (/en/register): name, email and password, or sign in with Google. After signing up, confirm your email through the link in your inbox before you can send a quote request.

Sign in on /en/login with your email and password, or Google. Forgot the password: open /en/forgot-password, enter your email and follow the reset link in the message. That link opens /en/reset-password.

Pottery households register on /en/register-household (see its own guide). A person invited to join an existing household accepts the invitation on /en/accept-invitation.

The Account page (/en/account) serves every role: personal details and email status, changing the password (other devices are signed out), the sign-in history and signing devices out, choosing which emails to receive, exporting personal data, and deleting the account with its data. A quick sign-out sits in the account menu on every page.

A locked account only sees a notice page. Passwords, citizen ID numbers and email addresses are private data: the Fox cannot see or change them, you do that yourself on the pages above.
