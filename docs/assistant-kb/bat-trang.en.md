# Bát Tràng pottery village

Bát Tràng is an old pottery village on the Red River, now in Bát Tràng commune, Hà Nội, about 15 km from the city centre. It has made ceramics for centuries and is known for blue-and-white, crackle and brown glazes.

Bát Tràng pottery is shaped, dried, painted, glazed and fired at about 1,200 to 1,300 °C. Today most households fire in gas kilns.

Visitors can go to showrooms and to workshops that welcome guests; the village map on vCraft shows which ones are open and their hours. The Bát Tràng ceramics market gathers many households' pieces.
