# How to use vCraft

vCraft is the digital exhibition and ordering platform of the Bát Tràng pottery village in Hà Nội. Every product is listed by exactly one pottery household, and every piece carries its own QR trace code.

As a guest (not signed in) you can browse the whole exhibition: the home page, the product catalogue, household profiles, the village map, the news, QR trace pages and the privacy policy. English uses its own paths, for example /en/products, /en/map, /en/news, /en/trace/CODE.

Signed-in areas: buyers manage quote requests on /en/quotes; households work in /en/studio (products, quotes, orders, profile); the village board uses /en/admin (approvals, coordination, progress, audit log). Every account has /en/account for profile details, changing the password, choosing email notifications and exporting or deleting personal data.

Accounts have three roles: buyer, household representative, and village administrator. Opening an area your role does not own shows a permission page.

Quick path for a buyer: create an account, browse products or send a bulk quote request, compare quotations, accept one, follow production and confirm delivery. A pottery household: register the household, get approved, publish products with a production journal, receive quote invitations and confirm its share of the order. The village board: reviews household applications, coordinates quote requests and publishes village news.
