# Buying on vCraft

Every product on vCraft is made and listed by one pottery household in Bát Tràng. The product page shows the price, how many are left, the material, the size and the names of the people who made it.

To buy a few pieces, press "Request a quote" on the product page or contact the household from its page. Prices are in Vietnamese dong; the English site also shows an approximate US dollar price.

A piece marked "One of a kind" is a single object: once sold it is gone. Batch products have several pieces of the same design.

For large orders (hundreds or thousands of pieces, a printed logo, corporate gifts), use a quote request.
