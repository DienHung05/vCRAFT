## Out of scope

- Social-network features such as follows, public comments, and a social feed. The agreed public experience is a digital exhibition; a later change in direction needs a new scoped effort.
- A pilot with real households and live wholesale orders. This release targets the course demonstration using representative sample data.
- Retail checkout, online payments, shipping integrations, electronic invoicing, and native mobile applications.
- Delivering the application, deploying services, collecting real identity documents, or executing database changes during this planning effort. Small local prototypes are allowed only to support a decision.
